Audit & Assurance

Independence and rigour that provides confidence and transparency for stakeholders and society — delivered through audits that look beyond compliance to genuine business insight.

Request a proposal What we offer
Overview

Confident decisions rest on numbers you can trust. Our assurance engagements bring objectivity, technical depth, and a stakeholder-first lens to every set of accounts.

We audit financial statements against applicable standards, test the controls behind the figures, and communicate findings in clear, actionable terms. The result is reporting that withstands scrutiny from boards, investors, lenders, and regulators alike.

From IND AS adoption to IFRS and US GAAP conversions, our specialists guide complex transitions while keeping disruption to your finance team to a minimum.

Why QuadIQ
  • Partner-led engagements with senior involvement
  • Deep IND AS, IFRS & US GAAP expertise
  • Risk-based, technology-enabled audit approach
  • Clear communication and timely delivery
